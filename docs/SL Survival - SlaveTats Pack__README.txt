SL Survival - SlaveTats Pack

SexLab Survival's own overlays (trauma wounds incl. spank marks, magic-curse collar, bikini curse, cum desperation)
as a SlaveTats pack, section "SL Survival", tattoo names "<texture name> [<Area>]". Female wounds, collar (face),
bikini curse (body/hands/feet) and cum overlays carry UBE variants under slavetats\UBE\ (baked from the CBBE /
vanilla-head originals with Grognak's UBE Tex Conversion Tool); SlaveTatsNG uses them for UBE races.
Male wounds have no UBE variant (the tool has female meshes only).
Used by "SL Survival - Integration Patch" (SLS_Utility routes overlays through SlaveTats when SlaveTatsNG, SlaveTats.esp
and this pack are present). Build: C:\Modding\MO2\work\UBE_tex_convert\build_packs.py

2026-10-19: the UBE set now covers every area of every SLS overlay. Added UBE copies of magic_silence_collar (Body,
Hands, Feet) and bikini_curse_body (Face), remapped from the 3BA textures with work\UBE_tex_convert\remap.py (colour
